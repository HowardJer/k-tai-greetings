About Me

While accounting has been the foundation of my career, my approach to work has always been driven by a desire to solve problems and improve existing systems and procedures.

I believe that a system or process does not have to be broken to warrant improvement. Evaluating existing methods and identifying opportunities to improve efficiency, accuracy and the use of available resources has been an integral part of my work throughout my career.

“Just because it works doesn't mean it can't be improved.”

Much of my knowledge beyond accounting has been acquired through necessity rather than formal training. When confronted with a challenge, I am willing to venture beyond my existing knowledge, learn what is needed and develop a practical solution.
